Etherana Coder is a powerful, open-source AI-integrated IDE designed to supercharge your development workflow. Built on top of VS Code, it brings state-of-the-art AI capabilities directly into your editor while giving you full control over your data and model selection.

## Key Features

- **AI Agents**: Deploy autonomous agents to handle complex coding tasks across your entire codebase.
- **Project Memory**: Local, persistent context that helps the AI understand your project's unique architecture and patterns.
- **Universal Model Support**: Connect to any AI provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, OpenRouter) or host your own models locally via Ollama.
- **Privacy First**: Your code stay yours. Messages are sent directly to providers without any intermediate data retention.
- **Checkpointing**: Automatically track and visualize AI-driven changes before they are applied.

## Getting Started

To get started with Etherana Coder, simply download the latest release and follow the onboarding steps to configure your preferred AI models.
